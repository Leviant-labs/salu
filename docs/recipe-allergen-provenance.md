# Starter Recipe Allergen Provenance

This document records the canonical EU Annex II (Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011) allergen declarations for the eight built-in starter recipes in `app/lib/src/features/plan/recipe_library.dart`, grounded in the ingredient records in `packages/salu_data/lib/src/catalogue/bundled_catalogue_data.dart`.

## Scope and Clinical Disclaimer

- **Listed ingredients only:** An explicit empty declaration (`{}`) means none of the 14 canonical allergen categories were identified in the listed generic ingredients during review.
- **No cross-contamination guarantee:** Neither `{}` nor any declared allergen set certifies absence of undeclared traces, shared processing lines, kitchen cross-contact, or brand-specific formulation differences. Salu does not provide clinical allergy suitability certification.
- **Fail-closed unknown handling:** Whenever a starter recipe includes a generic multi-ingredient or commercial reference entry whose full ingredient statement cannot be verified without a specific product label (such as commercially prepared bread or canned fish in unspecified oil/broth), `allergens` is intentionally left `null` (unknown) so strict allergy profiles reject the recipe rather than assume safety. Unverified entries are tracked below as release blockers if 100% starter coverage under strict allergy profiles is required.
